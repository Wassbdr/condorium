# Example input — Airbnb, as pitched in 2008

The point of this example: run a famously *rejected-by-everyone* idea, at its
initial stage, through the committee. A sycophantic AI calls it genius because
it knows the ending. A lazy AI kills it with "Craigslist exists". The
committee is supposed to do neither.

Run it:

```bash
panel eval --file examples/airbnb-2008.md --no-interview
```

---

Idea (as it was framed at the time — no hindsight):

Travelers visiting a city for a conference can't find affordable rooms when
hotels are sold out. Meanwhile locals have spare rooms or air mattresses.
We build a website where locals list their air bed + breakfast for conference
weekends, travelers book and pay online, and we take a cut of each booking.
We are three founders, two designers and one engineer, no funding, and we
plan to launch city by city following the conference calendar.
